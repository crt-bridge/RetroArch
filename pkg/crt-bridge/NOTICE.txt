crt-bridge emitter -- third-party components and licenses
===========================================================

This archive bundles the following components. The full license text for
each is in this LICENSES/ directory, under the filename listed after
"Text:" below. Every text was copied from the source tree this build was
made from (README.md gives the commit), either a license file of the
component or the notice at the head of one of its source files; none was
typed or rewritten for this archive. Where a component's own files carry
their notices in their headers, the text listed is the notice of one
file, and the other files of that component keep theirs.

Both builds carry every text listed here. A component marked "Windows
builds only" is compiled into the Windows executable and not into the
Linux one; its text is joined to both archives all the same.
